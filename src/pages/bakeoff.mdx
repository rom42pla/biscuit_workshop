# BISCUIT Bake-off 🍪
#### Best Paper Candidate Panel

The **BISCUIT Bake-off** is a two-phase live panel featuring the authors of this edition's spotlight papers, competing for the **Best Paper Award**.

- Phase 1: **Cross-fire**  
Each author gets a short slot to present the *impact* of their work, after which the other panelists challenge them on potential weaknesses, and they have one minute to respond, live.

- Phase 2: **Ethics round**   
The moderator poses provocative ethical questions tied to the field. 
Panelists respond with a quick yes/no, then explain their reasoning on the spot.

Expect a fast-paced, informal, and lively session: equal parts scientific scrutiny and audience entertainment 🍪
