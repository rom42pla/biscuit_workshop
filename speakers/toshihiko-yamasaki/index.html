Toshihiko Yamasaki

University of Tokyo

🌐 Website · 📜 Publications

Toshihiko Yamasaki is a Professor from the University of Tokyo whose research spans Computer Vision, Pattern Recognition, and Machine Learning, bridging foundational AI research and real-world applications. He leads projects on Attractiveness Computing, AI-assisted Profiling and Recommendation, and trustworthy AI, while actively collaborating with industry and academic partners to address large-scale societal challenges.
