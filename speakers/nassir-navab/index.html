Nassir Navab

Technical University of Munich

🌐 Website · 📜 Publications

Nassir Navab is a Professor from the Technical University of Munich specialized in computer-aided medical procedures and augmented reality. His work bridges medicine and computer science, focusing on technologies that enhance medical interventions. He has held key roles at MIT and Siemens and serves on the editorial boards of top medical imaging journals.
